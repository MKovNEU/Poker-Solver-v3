Preflop game

Hands that reach the flop are valued by all-in equity, adjusted for how well each realizes it: OOP gets OOP realization of IP's share in deep pots, and suited / connected hands more than offsuit gappers (playability). Both fade out towards SPR 1. Calls that would make the flop multiway aren't offered, so every flop is heads-up and can go straight to the postflop solver.
